# ATA chapter list

## Airframe and systems chapters

The ATA numbering convention groups aircraft systems by chapter. The chapters
relevant to this exercise are:

- **21** Air Conditioning and Pressurization
- **22** Auto Flight
- **23** Communications
- **24** Electrical Power
- **25** Equipment / Furnishings (not used here, included for context)
- **26** Fire Protection
- **27** Flight Controls
- **28** Fuel
- **29** Hydraulic Power
- **30** Ice and Rain Protection
- **31** Indicating / Recording Systems
- **32** Landing Gear
- **33** Lights
- **34** Navigation
- **35** Oxygen
- **36** Pneumatic
- **38** Water / Waste
- **49** Airborne Auxiliary Power
- **52** Doors

## Powerplant chapters

- **71** Power Plant (general)
- **72** Engine — Turbine / Turboprop
- **73** Engine Fuel and Control
- **74** Ignition
- **75** Air (engine)
- **76** Engine Controls
- **77** Engine Indicating
- **78** Exhaust
- **79** Oil
- **80** Starting

## Using the chapter numbers

A defect write-up is mapped to the ATA chapter of the affected system. For
example, a hydraulic low-pressure light is ATA 29, a brake wear pin is ATA 32,
an ILS receiver is ATA 34, an IDG/generator fault is ATA 24, a pack trip is ATA
21 and an APU start failure is ATA 49. The chapter number is the first key used
to find the MEL item; the item number then identifies the specific equipment
and its repair category. The chapter list is a classification aid, not an MEL
relief: an ATA chapter on its own never makes a defect deferrable.
