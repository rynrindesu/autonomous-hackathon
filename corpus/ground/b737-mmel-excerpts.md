# B737 MMEL illustrative excerpts

## ATA 21 — packs and temperature control

The MMEL lists **Pack Temperature Control System(s)** in ATA 21 with repair
category **C**, 4 installed and 2 required. An **(O)** procedure allows one
system (primary or standby) on each pack to be inoperative provided the
remaining system on the associated pack is verified to operate normally. Other
ATA 21 items cover outflow valves, duct overheat lights and trim-air valves, each
with its own category and (M)/(O) conditions. A pack trip is therefore not
automatically a no-go, but it must match an operator MEL item and its
conditions.

## ATA 24 — engine-driven generators and IDG

The MMEL lists **Engine Driven Generator Systems** with repair category **B**,
2 installed and 1 required. For the -600/-700/-800/-900 series an **(M)(O)**
relief may allow one generator to be inoperative, except for ETOPS, provided
the APU generator operates normally and is used throughout flight. Separate
items cover the APU generator and generator drive low-oil-pressure lights. The
operator MEL decides the exact wording and any additional conditions.

## ATA 29 — hydraulic pumps and pressure indication

**System B pumps** are listed with category **C**, 2 installed and 1 required.
Except for ETOPS, one pump may be inoperative provided the pressure indicator
operates normally and the thrust reversers operate normally. **System pressure
indications (A and B)** are category **C** with an **(O)** procedure: one may be
inoperative provided the associated system pressure is checked before each
departure and all hydraulic low-pressure lights operate normally.

## ATA 32 — brakes and antiskid

The **Antiskid System** is category **C**, 1 installed and 0 required, with an
**(M)(O)** procedure: it may be inoperative provided the associated antiskid
channel(s) is deactivated and operations are conducted in compliance with the
AFM. Parking brake and brake-light items have their own categories. Brake wear
limits themselves are maintenance limits, not an MEL dispatch relief; a brake
at the wear pin is normally a repair item.

## ATA 34 — ILS and navigation

Under the standby horizon indicator, **ILS Indication** is listed as category
**D**, 1 installed and 0 required, and may be inoperative. Navigation items such
as weather radar, ADF, VOR and DME have separate entries and conditions. An ILS
receiver failure on one side is not automatically a no-go, but the operator MEL
and the approach minima must be checked.

## ATA 49 — auxiliary power unit

The **Auxiliary Power Unit (APU)** is category **C**, 1 installed and 0
required. Except for ETOPS, it may be inoperative provided procedures do not
require its use. Variants with the APS2000 may have additional (M)(O)
conditions, including deactivation and inspection requirements. If the airport
or the flight needs APU air or power, the relief may not be usable.

## Caveat

These are paraphrased, illustrative excerpts from a public MMEL. They are not
an operator MEL and are not approved for dispatch. Always use the current
operator MEL, the aircraft configuration and the applicable airworthiness
directives.
