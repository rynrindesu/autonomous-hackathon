# 14 CFR Part 117 crew duty limits

## Flight duty period (FDP) limits

Part 117 limits the flight duty period (FDP) for unaugmented operations.
§117.13 says a certificate holder may not assign, and a crew member may not
accept, an FDP that will exceed the limits in **Table B to Part 117**. The table
depends on the scheduled start time (acclimated local time) and the number of
flight segments. For a flightcrew member who is not acclimated, the maximum FDP
is reduced by 30 minutes.

The FDP begins when the crew member reports for duty with the intention of
conducting a flight and ends when the aircraft is parked after the last flight
with no intention of further aircraft movement. Deadhead transportation is
duty and is not rest; for Table B it is not counted as a flight segment.

## Table B: maximum FDP by start time and segments

Representative Table B values for lineholders (hours). Columns are the number
of flight segments 1 through 7+:

| Start (acclimated) | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7+ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0000–0359 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9 |
| 0400–0459 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 9 | 9 | 9 |
| 0500–0559 | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 | 11.5 | 11 | 10.5 |
| 0600–0659 | 13 | 13 | 12 | 12 | 11.5 | 11 | 10.5 |
| 0700–1159 | 14 | 14 | 13 | 13 | 12.5 | 12 | 11.5 |
| 1200–1259 | 13 | 13 | 13 | 13 | 12.5 | 12 | 11.5 |
| 1300–1659 | 12 | 12 | 12 | 12 | 11.5 | 11 | 10.5 |
| 1700–2159 | 12 | 12 | 11 | 11 | 10 | 9 | 9 |
| 2200–2259 | 11 | 11 | 10 | 10 | 9 | 9 | 9 |
| 2300–2359 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 9 | 9 | 9 | 9 |

A delay that extends the FDP past the applicable table value is a hard
constraint unless an approved extension applies. The check should report the
planned FDP, the table limit and the start-time band.

## Flight time and cumulative limits

Table A to Part 117 sets maximum flight time for unaugmented operations by
report time: 8 hours for a report time of 0000–0459, 9 hours for 0500–1959, and
8 hours for 2000–2359. §117.23 sets cumulative limits, including 100 hours of
flight time in any 672 consecutive hours and 1,000 hours in any 365 consecutive
days. These are flight-time limits, not FDP limits; do not use one in place of
the other.

## Rest and unforeseen circumstances

§117.25 requires a rest period of at least 10 hours before a flight duty
period; the rest must include an opportunity for 8 hours of sleep and must
allow the crew to be fit for duty. If unforeseen operational circumstances
arise after takeoff beyond the certificate holder's control, a crew member may
exceed the flight-time limit only to the extent necessary to land safely at the
next destination or alternate, and the operator must report the exceedance.
